Privacy policy: Chat Export for Claude

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Chat Export for Claude is a Chrome extension that saves your own Claude conversations as files on your computer. It is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

What the extension reads

When you click the extension icon on a claude.ai tab, it asks Claude's website, using the session you are already signed in with, for the conversation you have open, or for the conversations you choose to export in bulk. It reads the conversation text, titles, dates and the names of attached files. It does not read anything on other websites, and it does not run in the background.

Where your data goes

  • Conversations go from Claude's website to your browser and from there to a file on your computer, your clipboard, or Chrome's print window. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • For a PDF export, the conversation is held briefly in Chrome's session storage on your device so the print page can show it, and is removed as soon as that page has loaded it.
  • Your export options are stored in Chrome's local storage on your device.
  • Exported HTML and PDF files never load remote images or scripts.
  • We run no server and collect no analytics, usage data or personal information.

Licence keys

If you buy Chat Export Pro, the purchase is handled by Gumroad, which acts as the seller and has its own privacy policy. When you enter your licence key, and about every two weeks after that, the extension sends the key and the product ID to Gumroad's licence service (api.gumroad.com) to check that it is valid. The key is stored in Chrome's local storage on your device. No conversation content is ever included in that request.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share user data. We do not use it for advertising, for creditworthiness, or for any purpose other than the extension's single function of exporting your conversations. No person reads your data.
