Privacy policy: Page to Markdown

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Page to Markdown is a Chrome extension that turns the web page you are looking at into Markdown text, which you can copy or save on your computer.

What the extension reads

When you click the extension icon, it reads the page in the current tab: the visible text and its structure (headings, lists, tables, code, links, image addresses), the page title and address, and, when the page provides them, the author's name and publication date. If you have selected text, it can read just the selection. It does not read anything when you have not clicked it, and it does not run in the background.

Batch export (a Pro feature) reads every tab in the current window in the same way. It needs Chrome's permission to read all websites, which the extension asks for only when you click "Save all as zip". You can refuse, and you can switch the permission off again in the popup ("Turn off") or on the extension's Details page in Chrome.

Where your data goes

  • The conversion happens inside your browser. The result goes to your clipboard or to a file on your computer that you choose to save. Page content is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Your options (front matter, remove links, remove images) are stored in Chrome's local storage on your device.
  • The extension does not load images or other files from the pages it converts; it only writes their addresses into the Markdown.
  • We run no server and collect no analytics, usage data or personal information.

Licence keys

If you buy Page to Markdown Pro, the purchase is handled by Gumroad, which acts as the seller and has its own privacy policy. When you enter your licence key, and about every two weeks after that, the extension sends the key and the product ID to Gumroad's licence service (api.gumroad.com) to check that it is valid. The key is stored in Chrome's local storage on your device. No page content is ever included in that request.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share user data. We do not use it for advertising, for creditworthiness, or for any purpose other than the extension's single function of converting web pages to Markdown for you. No person reads your data.
